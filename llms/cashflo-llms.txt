# CashFlo

> CashFlo is an India-based fintech platform that automates enterprise accounts payable, payments, GST compliance, vendor management, and supply-chain financing. It exposes a JWT-secured Data Ingestion API for pushing purchase orders and goods-receipt notes into the platform.

## APIs

- [CashFlo Data Ingestion API](https://developer.cashflo.io): Batch ingestion of purchase orders and goods-receipt notes (GRNs) with duplicate skipping and batch-error retrieval. OpenAPI 3.0.3, JWT auth.
